Watershed Based Approach

Using Watershed Based Approach, communities help to conserve biodiversity, restore ecosystems, and reduce the impact of climate change. The Green Initiative relies on its network of over 500 community groups to deliver its watershed based approach. At the grassroots level, Green Initiative's goal is to create climate resilient communities through restoration and protection of forest watersheds, creating sustainable livelihoods for communities across Africa.

Community Waste Collection

Community waste collection is a critical aspect of maintaining a clean and sustainable environment. Community waste collection is a fundamental practice that reflects our collective environmental responsibility, ensuring the proper disposal and management of waste to safeguard our ecosystems for future generations.

Planting Trees

The movement's initiatives include tree planting campaigns, waste management programs, and the promotion of renewable energy sources — with recent initiatives engaging in tree planting exercises. So far, 500,000 seedlings have been planted. Since the 1800s, human activities have been the main driver of climate change, primarily due to the burning of fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas. Burning fossil fuels generates greenhouse gas emissions that act like a blanket wrapped around the Earth, trapping the sun's heat and raising temperatures. The consequences of climate change now include intense droughts, water scarcity, severe fires, rising sea levels, flooding, melting polar ice, catastrophic storms and declining biodiversity.
